Built-in guardrails

Safe changes. Honest results.

GEO only changes your store when you say so, and it never makes things up.

You approve every change

Nothing goes live on your store until you click Approve. Autopilot is opt-in on Pro, and still skips anything risky.

Never makes things up

We only reword what’s already in your catalog. Missing details become a question for you, not a guess.

No health claims

Skin, hair and supplement products get a claims check first, in line with Australia’s TGA rules.

One-click undo

We save the old version of everything we change, so you can roll back in a second.

No fake reviews. Ever.

No fake reviews, no fake Reddit posts. Outreach is an honest note that you send yourself.

A cost cap per store

We track what every scan costs and stop before it goes over your plan.
