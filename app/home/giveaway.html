Questions about the giveaway

Who can enter?

Australian businesses that sell on Shopify. You must be 18 or over, and own or run the store (or have the owner’s OK). One entry per store.

What do I win?

3 months of GEO Done for you, worth A$3,000 (A$1,000 a month). Our team does all the work to get your store picked by AI, and you see the sales it brings.

How do you pick the winner?

At random, from every entry. We draw it at 12pm on Saturday 7 November, Perth time. We email and call the winner that day.

Does it cost anything?

No. It’s free to enter, and the 3 months are free. After that there’s no contract and nothing to pay, unless you want to keep going.

What do I need to do if I win?

Just the onboarding. You tell us about your store, we agree on what we can change, and you add one small code to Shopify. We help with all of it. Then we do the work.

When would my 3 months start?

On a date that suits you. We agree on it when we call.

My store isn’t on Shopify. Can I enter?

Not this time. GEO works with Shopify stores for now.

What happens to my details?

We use them to run the draw and to contact you about GEO. We never sell them. Read our privacy policy.

The rules. Short and simple.

  1. Who runs it. GEO (usegeo.app), run by Ross Delport in Perth, Western Australia.
  2. Who can enter. Australian businesses with an online store on Shopify. The person entering must be 18 or over and own or run the store, or have the owner’s OK. One entry per store. People who work for GEO, and their families, can’t enter.
  3. How to enter. Fill in the form on this page with your store’s website, your name, email and phone number. It’s free. You don’t need to buy anything.
  4. When. Entries open on Tuesday 3 November 2026 and close at 11:59pm on Friday 6 November 2026, Perth time (AWST). Late entries don’t count.
  5. The prize. One prize: 3 months of GEO Done for you, worth A$3,000 including GST (A$1,000 a month). It’s the work listed on this page. There’s no cash instead, and it can’t be swapped or given to another store. The 3 months start on a date we agree with the winner.
  6. The draw. We draw one winner at random from all entries at 12pm on Saturday 7 November 2026, Perth time, in Perth, Western Australia. We keep a record of the draw.
  7. Telling the winner. We email and call the winner on the day of the draw. We name the winning store on this page by Saturday 14 November 2026.
  8. If the winner doesn’t reply. If we can’t reach the winner, they don’t accept the prize by 5pm on Friday 13 November 2026 (Perth time), or their entry doesn’t meet these rules, we draw again at 12pm on Monday 16 November 2026, in the same way.
  9. After the 3 months. There’s nothing to pay and no contract. Keep going at A$1,000 a month only if you want to.
  10. Your details. We use them to run the giveaway and to contact you about GEO. We never sell them. See our privacy policy.
  11. Sharing results. With the winner’s OK, we may share how it went. Never without asking.
  12. Not run by Facebook. This giveaway isn’t run, backed or linked with Facebook, Instagram, Meta or Shopify.
  13. Changes. We only change or stop the giveaway if something outside our control means we have to, and only where the law allows.
