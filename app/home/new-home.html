What does GEO actually do?

It shows you whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity recommend your products, and who they pick instead. Then we do the work: we write buying guides and Reddit replies for you, and our team pitches the sites AI quotes most. It also counts the sales it can trace back to AI.

How do you know a sale came from AI?

You paste a small tracking code into Shopify once, which takes about 2 minutes. It spots shoppers who arrive from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and other AI assistants. Some AI apps hide where a shopper came from, so the real number is usually a bit higher. Google AI Overviews can’t be separated from normal Google, so we count them as search.

Can I pay to appear in AI answers?

Not inside ChatGPT’s answers. ChatGPT shows labelled ads below some answers, but OpenAI says ads can’t change what the answer says. Google sells ads in AI Overviews in many countries. GEO helps you earn the unpaid mentions.

Sources: OpenAI and Google Ads Help.

Will GEO change my store without asking?

No. GEO only reads what anyone can see on your store. Nothing in your Shopify admin changes, and you decide what to publish.

Do I need to install an app?

No. You sign in with your email and type your store’s address. To count sales from AI, you paste one small tracking code into Shopify.

How long does setup take?

About 5 minutes of your time. Your first check then runs by itself and takes a few minutes. You can close the page while it works.
