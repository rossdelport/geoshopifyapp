Where the wrong
prices come from.

Two pages say something different from your pricing page. AI reads both.

1

Your own Tenant Portal page

rentecdirect.com/details/tenant-portal says $45, twice: in the Quick Answer at the top and in the FAQ.

“…included with all Rentec Direct plans starting at $45/month.”
“The Tenant Portal is included at no additional cost with all Rentec Direct plans, starting at $45/month.”
2

Avail's accounting roundup

“The 10 Best Accounting Software Platforms for Landlords in 2026”, last updated June 23, 2026. Avail is part of Realtor.com. Gemini lists it as a source for its answer.

“Rentec Pro is $35/month, while Rentec PM is $40/month.”

ChatGPT's and Perplexity's answers don't show where their figures came from, so we don't claim to know.

Asked straight out,
they get it right.

“How much does Rentec Direct cost for a landlord with 8 rental properties?” ChatGPT said $25 Starter in 2 of 2 runs. So did Perplexity. Both read your pricing page.
