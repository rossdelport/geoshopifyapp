# Facts sheet for the redesign copy (checked 10 Oct 2026)

Every fact below was opened and read on 10 Oct 2026. Each one has: the source URL, the exact quote,
the date of the source, and a plain-English line we can use in copy (Australian spelling, no em dashes).
If a number is not on this sheet, don't put it on the site.

Rules for using these facts:

- Link every number to its source URL (honesty rule in CLAUDE.md).
- Say where the data comes from when it is not global. Adobe and Exploding Topics are **US** data.
  Triple Whale is **its own merchants**. None of these are Australia-only numbers.
- "Messages" are not "people". One shopper can send several messages.
- Never say AI growth was caused by GEO.

## 1. How many messages ChatGPT gets

### 1a. 2.5 billion a day (OpenAI, July 2025)

- **Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/21/chatgpt-users-send-2-5-billion-prompts-a-day/ (TechCrunch reporting OpenAI's statement to Axios)
- **Date:** 21 July 2025
- **Quote:** "ChatGPT receives 2.5 billion prompts from global users every day, OpenAI told Axios. About 330 million of those are coming from users in the U.S."
- **Copy line:** People send ChatGPT about 2.5 billion messages a day.

### 1b. 18 billion a week, and 2.6 billion a day (OpenAI's own research paper)

- **Source:** https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf ("How People Use ChatGPT", Chatterji et al., OpenAI / Harvard, also NBER Working Paper 34255: https://www.nber.org/papers/w34255)
- **Date:** 15 September 2025
- **Quote (introduction):** "By July 2025, 18 billion messages were being sent each week by 700 million users, representing around 10% of the global adult population."
- **Quote (Table 1, daily messages in millions):** "Jun 2025 ... Total Messages (M) 2,627". Caption: "Total daily counts are exact measurements of message volume from all consumer plans."
- **Maths:** 18 billion a week ÷ 7 = about 2.57 billion a day. Table 1 says 2.63 billion a day in June 2025. Both agree with the 2.5 billion figure.
- **Copy line:** By mid-2025, people were sending ChatGPT about 18 billion messages a week.

### 1c. Any newer official daily figure in 2026?

**No.** I found no OpenAI statement of daily or weekly message volume in 2026. OpenAI's 2026 "Signals"
pages (https://openai.com/signals/data/, https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-adoption-has-expanded/)
give shares and trends, not totals. Blogs quoting "2.5 billion a day in 2026" are re-using the July 2025
number. **Use the 2025 figures and label them "2025".** We can pair them with the 2026 user numbers in
section 3 so readers see the growth themselves.

## 2. Share of ChatGPT messages about things to buy ("Purchasable Products")

### What the paper says

- **Source:** https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf
- **Date:** 15 September 2025
- **Definition quote (Appendix A, the classifier prompt):** "purchasable_products: Inquiries about products or services available for purchase."
- **Example messages the paper gives for this category:** "iPhone 15." "What's the best streaming service?" "How much are Nikes?" "Recommend a good laptop under $1000."
- **Where the number is:** Figure 9 ("Breakdown of granular conversation topic shares ... Each bin reports a percentage of the total population. Shares are calculated from a sample of approximately 1.1 million sampled conversations from May 15, 2024 through June 26, 2025."). The figure is an image, so its numbers are not in the PDF text. I could not read the bar labels directly (I fetched both the OpenAI and NBER PDFs as text; the figure values don't come through).

### Confirmation of the percentage (two independent sources citing the paper)

- **eMarketer**, Rachel Wolff, 24 October 2025: https://www.emarketer.com/content/chatgpt-minimal-influence-on-ecommerce-sales-for-now
  - **Quote:** "Just 2.1% of ChatGPT conversations involve “purchasable products,” according to OpenAI’s own research, slightly more than the share related to “greetings and chitchat.”"
- **Marketplace Pulse**: https://www.marketplacepulse.com/articles/chatgpts-instant-checkout-is-an-instant-threat (published around the Instant Checkout launch, late September 2025)
  - **Quote:** "OpenAI research shows 2.1% of ChatGPT conversations involve purchasable products."
- Sanity check: the paper's text quotes other Figure 9 values (Tutoring or Teaching 10.2%, How-To Advice 8.5%, Computer Programming 4.2%, Relationships and Personal Reflection 1.9%), so these sources are reading the same chart.

**Status: 2.1% is confirmed by two reliable secondary sources that cite the paper, not read by me off the
figure itself.** It is a share of **messages** (weighted by message volume), averaged over May 2024 to
June 2025, consumer plans, adults only. It covers products **and services**.

### Shopping questions per day (our estimate, show the working)

| Daily messages used | × 2.1% | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2.5 billion (OpenAI, July 2025) | 0.021 | **about 52 million a day** |
| 2.57 billion (18 billion a week ÷ 7, paper) | 0.021 | about 54 million a day |
| 2.63 billion (Table 1, June 2025) | 0.021 | about 55 million a day |

**Safest wording:** "roughly 50 million questions a day about things to buy, on ChatGPT alone (2025)".
Round down to 50 million. Say "questions" or "messages", not "shoppers" or "people". Label it as our
estimate from OpenAI's numbers.

- **Copy line:** OpenAI's own research found about 2% of ChatGPT messages are about things to buy. At 2.5 billion messages a day, that's roughly 50 million shopping questions a day, on ChatGPT alone.
- **Footnote line:** Our estimate: 2.5 billion messages a day (OpenAI, July 2025) × 2.1% about products or services to buy (OpenAI research, 2024 to 2025).

## 6. Does ChatGPT show ads? (for "you can't buy a recommendation")

**Yes, ChatGPT now shows ads, including in Australia and New Zealand.** But they sit below the answer, are
labelled, and OpenAI says advertisers cannot change the answer itself.

- **Source (OpenAI Help Center):** https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt (read 10 Oct 2026)
  - **Quotes:** "Ad testing started in the United States on February 9, 2026." "Ads may appear for users on the Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu accounts will not have ads." "No. Ads do not influence ChatGPT’s answers. Ads run on separate systems from our chat model, and advertisers have no ability to shape, rank, or alter ChatGPT’s responses. Ads are separate and clearly labeled." "Ads can appear below the end of a response. Ads are clearly labeled as sponsored and visually separated from ChatGPT’s response."
- **Source (OpenAI, Australia confirmed):** https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-southeast-asia-taiwan/
  - **Date:** 23 September 2026
  - **Quotes:** "This announcement follows Asia Pacific launches in Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea and India" and "Ads in ChatGPT are always clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT’s answers, and advertising does not influence the answers ChatGPT provides."
- **Source (OpenAI ads principles):** https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/ (January 2026)
  - **Quote:** "Answer independence: Ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you. Answers are optimized based on what’s most helpful to you. Ads are always separate and clearly labeled."

**Google is different. Ads can appear inside AI Overviews in Australia and New Zealand.**

- **Source (Google Ads Help):** https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16297775?hl=en (read 10 Oct 2026)
  - **Quotes:** "Ads are eligible to be shown above, below or within AI Overviews on Search" and "Ads in AI Overviews are currently available in English on mobile and desktop devices in the following countries: Australia, Canada, India, Indonesia, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, and US."

**Accurate ways to say it:**

- OK: "ChatGPT has ads now, but they sit below the answer and are labelled. OpenAI says advertisers can't change what the answer recommends. That part has to be earned."
- OK: "The recommendation inside the answer isn't for sale. It's earned from what AI can read about your products."
- OK (headline idea stays fair): "The free sales channel your store is missing", because the organic recommendation costs nothing per click. When talking about GEO itself, say "no ad spend", not "free".
- **Don't say:** "AI assistants don't have ads", "ChatGPT is ad-free", or "you can't pay to appear in Google's AI answers" (Google sells ad slots inside AI Overviews in AU/NZ).
